## About this course

The course is structured as a sequence of programming tasks rather than a sequence of lessons. Each task isolates one part of the language and makes you use it until the syntax is automatic: operators, control flow, loops, functions, collections and the algorithms that sit on top of them.

Because the exercises are drawn from both traditional problem sets and real interview questions, the course doubles as preparation for a C# developer role. Everything is checked automatically, so you always know whether your solution is correct before moving on.
