# A life spent exploring through the code, commerce and the road.

Photographer · Educator · Author · Software engineer

![Sergey Romanenko, photographed in Hoi An](https://sergeyromanenko.com/about/sergey-romanenko.jpg)

Hoi An, Vietnam

## Sergey Romanenko

I am a software engineer and independent author of programming courses on Stepik, with more than 17 years of commercial software development experience across application architecture, web development, automation, and testing.

Since 2022, I have been creating practical programming courses in SQL, JavaScript, Python, Go, PHP, Kotlin, and other subjects. My courses combine structured explanations, exercises, and verifiable assignments, helping learners develop practical skills and work independently with each technology.

I have contributed to commercial and open-source software projects, previously worked as a mentor at Solvery.io, and authored technical books and learning materials on SQL and Markdown. Alongside software engineering and education, I pursue photography and develop independent creative projects.

I’ve spent my life moving between disciplines, places, and ways of understanding the world. I began with computers and software, learning to take complicated systems apart and understand how they work. Over time, that curiosity expanded beyond the screen into teaching, photography, independent projects, and the experience of living and travelling across different places and cultures.

Today, I work across code, cameras, and education, while continuing to explore the world around me. I build software and practical tools, create learning resources, and photograph people, streets, and everyday life. Some of my work is technical and structured; some is personal, observational, and open-ended. What connects it is a desire to understand things firsthand, make something meaningful from what I discover, and share the result with others.

How it connects

## Different disciplines. A shared *curiosity about how* the world works.

01

### Engineering — Understanding How Things Work

My work in software has taught me to approach complicated problems with curiosity, patience, and a willingness to look beneath the surface. I work with application architecture, frontend development, APIs, testing, and independent technical projects. I continue to explore software through research and development, building practical solutions and investigating better ways to solve real problems.

02

### Education — Making Knowledge Useful

I turn technical experience into learning resources that help people understand concepts and apply them independently. Through programming courses, practical exercises, books, documentation, and mentoring, I work to make complex subjects more approachable without stripping away the details that matter.

03

### Photography — Paying Attention to People and Place

Photography gives me a way to explore the world through direct observation. I photograph people, streets, architecture, and everyday situations, with a growing focus on travel, street, portrait, and documentary photography. My personal projects explore the quieter and more complicated sides of human experience: belonging, distance, memory, and the feeling of trying to find a place in the world.

04

### Independent Creation — Building and Sharing

Alongside my client work and teaching, I develop open-source software, publish practical resources, and build small tools that solve specific problems. I value independent work that can be examined, reused, improved, and shared. These projects give me room to experiment, follow an idea beyond its initial purpose, and make something useful available to other people.

  I combined freelance event photography with model scouting for Liberty and Sigma Model Agency, developing my photographic skills while working closely with models and aspiring talent. 3. 2010–2020
  #### Software Engineering & Contract Development
  I worked in software engineering and frontend development for an international print-on-demand company, progressing from Software Engineer to Senior Frontend Developer. My responsibilities evolved from core software development to building and maintaining customer-facing web interfaces and supporting the company’s web infrastructure. I focused on frontend architecture, user interface development, reusable components, responsive design, performance optimization, API integrations and cross-system compatibility. Working in the print-on-demand and e-commerce industry gave me experience with the technical challenges of digital platforms connecting online customer experiences with complex business and production workflows. I also contributed to software quality, testing, automation, localization and technical documentation, helping maintain reliable and consistent user experiences across the company’s web ecosystem. Alongside my primary role, I pursued independent development and contract projects across different areas of software engineering.

My coursework included cryptography and encryption, as well as computer graphics programming in C and C++. My graduation project involved developing a content management system (CMS). I also completed training in Cisco networking technologies. Alongside my formal education, I worked with a broad range of programming languages and technologies, including C, C++, C#, Java, x86 assembly, PHP, JavaScript, Delphi and Free Pascal. I explored early web development and software engineering, and founded and ran the PascalGames community, publishing resources related to Delphi, Free Pascal and game development.
