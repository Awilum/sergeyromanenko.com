# Professional Mentoring at Solvery

Supported aspiring developers as they improved their programming skills and worked toward
their professional goals in the rapidly evolving IT industry.

Mentor · Solvery · 2022–2023 · Individualized mentoring · Web development, software engineering and career development

02 · My Role as a Mentor

## What I did at Solvery.

For many years, my professional work focused on developing, maintaining, and testing projects of varying complexity. During this time, I gained extensive experience with a wide range of programming languages and technologies.

From 2022 to 2023, I worked as a mentor on Solvery, supporting aspiring developers as they improved their programming skills and worked toward their professional goals in the rapidly evolving IT industry.

My role covered a broad range of responsibilities, from providing recommendations and practical advice to teaching different aspects of programming. My goal was to help each mentee unlock their potential and support them throughout their learning and professional development.

My approach was based on personalized mentoring tailored to each individual's needs. I provided detailed guidance based on my practical software development experience, along with ongoing support to help mentees overcome challenges and make consistent progress.

This mentoring experience strengthened my ability to inspire and guide people at the beginning of their careers, helping them gain the knowledge, practical skills, and confidence needed to move forward.

- **Role:** Mentor
- **Platform:** Solvery
- **Period:** 2022–2023
- **Format:** Individual mentoring

03 · Who I Helped

## How I Could Help

My mentoring was aimed at beginners, self-taught developers, people who had completed programming courses, and junior specialists looking to strengthen their practical skills.

I could help mentees:

- Understand and work with real-world development projects.
- Improve their technical skills and build practical experience.
- Create a portfolio and develop personal projects.
- Prepare for technical interviews.
- Build a professional resume and portfolio.
- Create a structured learning and career development plan.
- Understand modern web development and software testing.
- Review code and learn professional development practices.
- Create testing documentation, including test cases, checklists, and bug reports.
- Develop responsive websites for different screen sizes and devices.
- Develop and test APIs.
- Choose a suitable direction for further development in IT.
- Strengthen their technical foundations through individualized mentoring.
