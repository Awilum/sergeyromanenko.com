Professional Mentoring at Solvery

Supported aspiring developers as they improved their programming skills and worked toward their professional goals in the rapidly evolving IT industry.

Mentor · Solvery · 2022–2023 · Individualized mentoring · Web development, software engineering and career development

02 · My Role as a Mentor

What I did at Solvery.

For many years, my professional work focused on developing, maintaining, and testing projects of varying complexity. During this time, I gained extensive experience with a wide range of programming languages and technologies.

From 2022 to 2023, I worked as a mentor on Solvery, supporting aspiring developers as they improved their programming skills and worked toward their professional goals in the rapidly evolving IT industry.

My role covered a broad range of responsibilities, from providing recommendations and practical advice to teaching different aspects of programming. My goal was to help each mentee unlock their potential and support them throughout their learning and professional development.

My approach was based on personalized mentoring tailored to each individual's needs. I provided detailed guidance based on my practical software development experience, along with ongoing support to help mentees overcome challenges and make consistent progress.

This mentoring experience strengthened my ability to inspire and guide people at the beginning of their careers, helping them gain the knowledge, practical skills, and confidence needed to move forward.

03 · Who I Helped

How I Could Help

My mentoring was aimed at beginners, self-taught developers, people who had completed programming courses, and junior specialists looking to strengthen their practical skills.

I could help mentees:

  • Understand and work with real-world development projects.
  • Improve their technical skills and build practical experience.
  • Create a portfolio and develop personal projects.
  • Prepare for technical interviews.
  • Build a professional resume and portfolio.
  • Create a structured learning and career development plan.
  • Understand modern web development and software testing.
  • Review code and learn professional development practices.
  • Create testing documentation, including test cases, checklists, and bug reports.
  • Develop responsive websites for different screen sizes and devices.
  • Develop and test APIs.
  • Choose a suitable direction for further development in IT.
  • Strengthen their technical foundations through individualized mentoring.

05 · Student Testimonials

What My Mentees Said

Real reviews from my Solvery mentoring profile, reproduced in full with the original names.

  • 01

    Bogdan

    I want to become a frontend developer. Before joining Solvery, I had a basic understanding of the profession and had completed a couple of short courses to get a general idea of how everything worked.

    My main goal was to go from zero to an internship at a company or getting my first freelance projects.

    During our sessions, the mentor helped me organize the knowledge I already had and outline a plan for further learning. Since we started working together, I have already built two personal projects, and we are continuing to improve them.

    After having regular sessions with mentor Sergey Romanenko, I have noticed several benefits for myself (some specific to Sergey, and others related to mentoring in general :))

    1. A ‘therapeutic’ effect

    In 2023, people trying to enter the IT industry (myself included, hello 👋🏼) were becoming increasingly uncertain about their prospects.

    There were fears about AI, negative news about thousands of developers being laid off by leading companies, a feeling that the market was becoming oversaturated, and concerns about how difficult it would be to find a job.

    Sergey has been working in the industry for a long time and understands the actual situation. He helps me put my concerns about future employment and related issues into perspective.

    His advice is practical and specific, rather than the usual 'study hard and work hard'. He discusses particular technologies and strategies that can genuinely improve your chances of getting hired.

    2. Motivation

    Many people find it difficult to stay motivated while learning for various reasons: challenging material, losing interest, and other obstacles. Having a mentor gives you an additional incentive to keep learning and work through problems as they arise.

    When I was taking courses, I often had to force myself to sit down and study because so many things were unclear.

    Now, after every call with Sergey, I get a new burst of energy that helps me keep moving forward :)

    3. Practical knowledge

    Sergey often shares professional tips and details about real development workflows that are rarely covered in popular courses. And I have several courses to compare them with :)

    Sergey teaches me to do things properly from the beginning, so I don't waste time making mistakes that could have been avoided.

    Overall, it's great. I really like it.
