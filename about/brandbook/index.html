A visual identity shaped by travel and everyday life.

This is not a corporate brand guideline. It is a record of where the colours, type and feeling of this website actually come from: the places I lived, the roads I rode, and the objects I carry.

Visual identity · Travel · Everyday life

01 · Indonesia

Where the palette began.

I spent time living in Indonesia. The palette of this website is not a fashionable choice made at a desk. It is the colour of a period of my life.

During that period I was surrounded by a particular combination of natural colours and textures: warm earth and soil, sand and sandy paths, brown and ochre tones, tropical green vegetation, olive and forest green, warm white and natural beige, sunlight and warm yellow accents.

These colours gradually became more than colours I liked. They became part of my everyday environment, my clothes, the accessories I carried, and the way I saw the world around me. Eventually this aesthetic became part of the visual language of my website.

I did not simply select a palette. It reflects a real chapter of my life and a personal aesthetic shaped by living and travelling in Indonesia. The identity should communicate exploration, travel and freedom, a connection with nature, simplicity and practicality, warmth and authenticity, and a personal, adventurous lifestyle.

02 · Colour

The colours I live in.

Earth and soil, sand, tropical vegetation, warm sunlight, natural fabrics. Each colour on this site has a job; none is decorative filler.

The palette is an earth family. It is the same family I wore, carried and walked through in Indonesia, translated into the small, precise set of tokens the website actually uses. No new colours were invented for this page.

03 · EIGER Adventure

An Indonesian brand that became part of everyday exploration.

During my time in Indonesia, EIGER became one of the brands I genuinely connected with. I appreciated its adventurous character, practical equipment, and distinctive outdoor aesthetic.

EIGER is an Indonesian outdoor and adventure equipment brand. Some of its accessories became part of my everyday life — waist bags and other travel accessories I purchased and used in Indonesia. Their earthy colours, including green, brown, sand and yellow, became part of the visual environment I associate with that period. More than objects, they remind me of the places I explored and the kind of lifestyle I wanted to embrace.

A concise, factual introduction to the company, drawn from its own historical materials: EIGER was born in Indonesia in 1989. Its founder is Ronny Lukito. The company's early history grew out of bag manufacturing, beginning modestly with two sewing machines and limited initial resources. The name EIGER was inspired by Mount Eiger in the Swiss Alps. The company's own timeline distinguishes the brand's birth in 1989 from the formal establishment of Eigerindo and its first Cihampelas store in 1995.

EIGER is a personal source of inspiration, not an official partner or sponsor of this website. Its identity is not imitated here; it is one of the things that shaped how I see colour, equipment and travel.

References: eigeradventure.com · eigerindo.co.id/About/history

04 · Yamaha PG-1

Yellow motorcycle, green bandana, freedom of movement.

My yellow Yamaha PG-1, with a green bandana tied to its handlebars, is a personal symbol of exploration. It is part of my everyday travel lifestyle.

Yellow represents sunlight, warmth, energy and movement. Green represents nature, tropical landscapes and exploration. Together they are the most direct expression of the aesthetic I live in: the sun and the jungle, the road and the vegetation beside it.

The motorcycle is how I take smaller roads, discover new places and experience everyday life. It represents the freedom to explore at my own pace. These personal objects relate to the broader visual identity of the website: the same yellow and green reappear in the palette, the same practicality in the design, the same sense of movement in the way the pages are composed.

05 · Personal style

Clothing, bags, accessories, and everyday aesthetics.

This aesthetic is not limited to the website. It is reflected in how I dress and the accessories I choose.

My everyday visual style includes earthy brown tones, olive and natural green, sand and beige, warm white, yellow accents, and practical travel-oriented accessories. My clothing, bags, motorcycle and website all belong to the same visual world. The website's design is an extension of my personality and everyday lifestyle, rather than an artificially constructed corporate identity.

06 · Typography

Three font-families, each with one job.

Instrument Serif for display and personal voice; Inter for body and interface; IBM Plex Mono for measurement and marginalia.

Display · Instrument Serif 400

A visual identity shaped by travel.

High-contrast, tight, old-style. Carries headlines, page titles, chapter openings and personal emphasis. Never bold. Italic is for personal voice and emphasis, not for section titles.

Secondary · Instrument Serif 400

Where the palette began.

Section and chapter headings inside the page.

Body · Inter 400–600

Calm, readable, set at a measure rather than the page width. Navigation, body copy and controls. The serif says who is speaking; Inter carries the explanation.

Marginalia · IBM Plex Mono 400

PLATE 07 / 24 · Hoi An, Vietnam · 2026

The field-note layer: dates, locations, counts, formats, plate numbers. Always meta or micro size, in label or muted colour, with tabular numerals. Never prose, never display.

Long-form prose is set at a 34–44rem measure. A page may contain both a display moment and a micro mono moment. Roughly one mono annotation per composition unit.

Closing

The website is an extension of the way I experience the world.

The design of this website is not an arbitrary collection of colours and fonts. It is a visual representation of the places I have lived, the objects I carry, the roads I travel, and the person I have become. Warm paper, photographs, ruled lines, quiet type — and underneath all of it, the sand and the green of South East Asia.
