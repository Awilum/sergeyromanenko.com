IT Books

2 published works — Free to read

Two books, both free to read online and free to download, both open source. The text lives in a public repository alongside the build process, so corrections are possible and the books cannot be taken offline.

They are written for someone starting from nothing, and they take the order of ideas seriously — the sequence in which something has to be understood before it can be used, and the wrong turns a beginner is most likely to take. That ordering is the part that is hard to improvise, and the reason these exist as books rather than as a list of links.
