# Currency Converter

Convert between currencies using reference exchange rates from a reputable public data source, with the rate and its date shown.

## At a glance

- **Currencies:** Reference rates
- **Data source:** open.er-api
- **Network:** On demand
- **Offline result:** No

## What you can do

1. **Reference rates**
  Exchange rates from a public data source, shown with the date they were published.
2. **Swap currencies**
  Reverse the conversion in one click.
3. **Searchable lists**
  Pick from the supported currencies by code or name.
4. **Runs in your browser**
  Only the currency pair is requested; your amount stays on your device.

## How it works

Currency Converter

Compare money across borders with reference exchange rates, so you can sanity-check a price before you pay it. The rate comes from a public provider and is shown with the date it was published. Nothing you type is sent beyond the currency pair being converted.

Everything runs in your browser. The selected currencies and the amount stay on your device; only the currency pair is used to fetch a rate.

## What it shows

The converted amount, the exchange rate used, and the date of that rate. Swap the two currencies with one click, or enter a new amount and the result updates immediately.

## Read the rate, do not trust it blindly

The rate on screen is a market reference rate, not the rate a bank, card issuer or exchange office will apply. Those add spreads and fees, so treat the number as a comparison point rather than a promise.

## Accuracy and limits

Rates are published on business days. Weekends and holidays usually repeat the previous business day’s rate. When the source cannot be reached, the converter tells you rather than showing a guess.
