# Unit Converter

Convert length, weight, temperature, volume, area, speed, fuel economy and data transfer rates between metric, US and Imperial units.

## At a glance

- **Categories:** 8
- **Systems:** Metric, US, Imperial
- **Network requests:** None
- **Offline support:** Yes

## What you can do

1. **Eight categories**
  Length, weight, temperature, volume, area, speed, fuel economy and data transfer rates.
2. **US and Imperial kept distinct**
  US customary and Imperial gallons are separate units, so a gallon is never ambiguous.
3. **Reciprocal fuels**
  Miles per gallon convert to litres per 100 km correctly.
4. **Runs offline**
  Every conversion is calculated locally with no network request.

## How it works

Unit Converter

Change a quantity from one unit to another in a single step. All eight categories, including temperature and fuel economy, are calculated locally without a network request.

US customary and Imperial volumes are listed separately so a “gallon” never means two different things. Fuel economy handles the reciprocal conversions, so miles per gallon becomes litres per 100 kilometres correctly.
