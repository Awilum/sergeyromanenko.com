# Time Difference Calculator

Compare local times, daylight, and time differences across the cities that matter wherever you travel.

## What you can do

1. **Compare times across cities**
  See local times for every city you add at a single moment, with offsets and day/night status.
2. **Track daylight hours**
  See sunrise, sunset, and day length for each city on the selected date.
3. **Switch time formats**
  Toggle between AM/PM and 24-hour formats; your preference is remembered.
4. **Quick city sets**
  Add the 8 city groups — Europe, The Americas, Southeast Asia, Japan & Korea and more — with one click.
5. **Works offline**
  All calculations run locally in your browser — no network required once loaded.

## How it works

Time Difference Calculator for travellers

Compare local times across every city you care about at a single moment. The tool reads your own time zone and shows what that means everywhere else — the local time, how far ahead or behind it is, whether it is day or night there right now, and when the sun rises and sets on the day you are looking at.

Each city gets its own day timeline, drawn from the real sunrise and sunset for that city on that date. Daylight hours genuinely change with the season and the latitude, so a June afternoon in Lisbon and a June afternoon in Singapore are not the same length, and neither is the same as either of them in December.

Sunrise, sunset and the periods around them are calculated on your device from the city’s coordinates using standard astronomical formulae. There is no lookup service to call, nothing to wait for, and nothing that stops working when you are offline or on a slow connection.

Times are written in AM/PM by default, with 24-hour and combined formats available, and your choice is remembered. Conversion uses the IANA time zone database, so daylight saving changes, historical rules and non-hour offsets such as India and Nepal are handled for you.

The tool is served from this website and needs no installation — it is available on any device with a browser.
