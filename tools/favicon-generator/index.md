# Favicon Generator

Generate favicons and app icons in your browser from text or an image. Export PNGs, a multi-resolution ICO, ZIP, manifest, and HTML tags. Nothing is uploaded.

## At a glance

- **Icon sizes:** 6
- **Exports:** PNG · ICO · ZIP
- **Network requests:** None
- **Offline support:** Yes

## What you can do

1. **Text or image source**
  Generate from a short text label or an uploaded image, with no editing required.
2. **Shape and color options**
  Choose square, rounded, or circle shapes with custom text and background colors.
3. **Live preview**
  Preview every asset on light and dark backgrounds before exporting.
4. **ZIP, manifest and HTML**
  Download a ZIP with icons, site.webmanifest, and copyable HTML tags.
5. **Runs entirely in your browser**
  All processing and export are local; nothing is uploaded.

## How it works

The Favicon Generator

Generate a complete favicon set entirely in your browser from text or an image. Every generated file stays on your device.

## How it works

Type a short label or upload an image, choose a shape, then download the assets individually, as a ZIP, or copy the HTML snippet.

## Privacy

Images and generated assets are processed locally. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or logged. Only files you explicitly download are written.

## Limits

Very large images may be constrained by browser memory. Canvas-based processing does not preserve EXIF or ICC metadata. Transparency is preserved for PNG output. ICO files contain PNG-encoded icon images.
