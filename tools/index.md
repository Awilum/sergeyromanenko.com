These tools grew out of things I needed while studying at university, working, and building my own projects. Over time, I found myself solving the same kinds of everyday problems and building small tools to make the work easier. Eventually, I decided to bring them together in one place and make them available to everyone. Each tool runs directly in your browser, with no installation or account required.
