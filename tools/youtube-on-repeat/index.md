# YouTube on Repeat

Paste a YouTube URL and loop the whole video or a precise A–B section, with playback-speed control and keyboard shortcuts. Runs entirely in your browser.

## At a glance

- **Repeat modes:** 3
- **Speed presets:** 8
- **Network requests:** Playback only
- **Offline support:** No

## What you can do

1. **Whole-video and A–B looping**
  Repeat an entire video or a precise section between two markers.
2. **Playback speed control**
  Speeds from 0.25× to 2× for study and listening.
3. **Keyboard shortcuts**
  Space, arrows, A, B and Escape.
4. **No account or upload**
  Looping and speed control stay on your device; only YouTube serves the video.

## How it works

YouTube on Repeat

Paste a YouTube link and the video loads in an embedded player. Loop the whole video, or set A and B markers to repeat a precise section — for a chorus, a phrase in another language, or a step in a tutorial. Playback speed runs from 0.25× to 2×.

## How it works

The player is YouTube’s own embedded player, loaded through the official IFrame API. Looping is implemented here by watching playback time and seeking back to the start marker when the end marker is reached; nothing is downloaded, proxied, or re-encoded, and the video stays where YouTube hosts it.

## Repeat modes

Three modes: Off (normal playback), Repeat Video (restarts at the end), and Repeat Section (loops between the A and B markers). The active mode is always shown.

## A–B loop

Set A at the current position, set B at a later position. Clear Loop removes both. Preview Loop jumps to A and starts playing. Markers are validated against the video duration and against each other.

## Privacy

The video URL and your loop settings stay in this browser. Recent-video history, if used, is stored in localStorage on your device only. Nothing is sent to any server other than the YouTube playback itself.
