# Image Cropper

Crop images in your browser. Choose freeform or fixed aspect ratios, rotate and flip, zoom, preview, and download the result. Nothing is uploaded.

## At a glance

- **Aspect ratios:** 7
- **Export formats:** PNG · JPEG · WebP
- **Network requests:** None
- **Offline support:** Yes

## What you can do

1. **Freeform and fixed aspect ratios**
  Crop freely or lock to 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 4:5, 16:9 and original proportions.
2. **Rotate, flip and zoom**
  Rotate and flip the image, zoom and reposition, then export the selected area.
3. **Preview before export**
  See crop dimensions in pixels and download PNG, JPEG or WebP.
4. **Runs entirely in your browser**
  Cropping and export are local; nothing is uploaded.

## How it works

The Image Cropper

Crop an image entirely in your browser. The source file, the crop area and the exported image never leave your device.

## How it works

Upload an image, drag the crop area, choose an aspect ratio, rotate or flip if needed, then download the cropped result in PNG, JPEG or WebP.

## Privacy

Images are processed locally. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or logged. Only the file you explicitly download is written.

## Limits

Very large images may be constrained by browser memory. Canvas-based export does not preserve EXIF or ICC metadata. Transparency is preserved for PNG and WebP; JPEG output uses a solid background.
