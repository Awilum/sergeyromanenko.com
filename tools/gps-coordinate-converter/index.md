# GPS Coordinate Converter

Convert GPS coordinates between decimal degrees, degrees and decimal minutes, and degrees, minutes and seconds, with validation of ranges.

## At a glance

- **Formats:** 3
- **Datum:** WGS84
- **Network requests:** None
- **Offline support:** Yes

## What you can do

1. **Three formats**
  Decimal degrees, degrees and decimal minutes, and degrees, minutes and seconds.
2. **Validated ranges**
  Latitude, longitude and minutes and seconds are checked before conversion.
3. **Hemispheres kept straight**
  Cardinal directions and signs are preserved through every conversion.
4. **Runs offline**
  No map, geocoding or network request is needed.

## How it works

GPS Coordinate Converter

Move a coordinate between the three formats GPS devices and maps disagree about: decimal degrees, degrees with decimal minutes, and degrees, minutes and seconds. Ranges are validated, so an out-of-range latitude or a bad minute value is rejected instead of silently mangled.

All inputs are assumed to be WGS84 geographic coordinates. This tool converts between representations of the same coordinate; it does not convert between geographic datums or map projections.
