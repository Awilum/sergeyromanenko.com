# Monoalphabetic Substitution

Create your own substitution alphabet — perfect for encoding travel memories and personal journey symbols with a custom 26-letter mapping.

## At a glance

- **Key length:** 26 letters
- **Validation:** Permutation
- **Formatting:** Preserved
- **Offline support:** Yes

## What you can do

1. **Custom alphabet**
  Replace A–Z with any permutation of 26 letters.
2. **Key validation**
  The key must be a complete permutation.
3. **Formatting preserved**
  Case and non-letters stay in place.
4. **Runs offline**
  Everything runs in your browser.

## How it works

## Monoalphabetic Substitution Cipher

Monoalphabetic substitution creates a free-choice mapping between plaintext and ciphertext alphabets. Each plaintext letter maps to a unique ciphertext letter, creating a personalized code for travel memories.

## The Mathematics

**Encryption:** `E(x) = alphabet[x]` where `alphabet` is a 26-letter permutation of A-Z

**Decryption:** `D(y) = ALPHABET[position of y in alphabet]`

Every plaintext letter maps to exactly one ciphertext letter, and vice versa.
