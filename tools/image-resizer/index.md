# Image Resizer

Resize images in your browser by pixels or percentage, with aspect-ratio lock and high-quality resampling. Preview and download the result. Nothing is uploaded.

## At a glance

- **Resize modes:** 3
- **Export formats:** PNG · JPEG · WebP
- **Network requests:** None
- **Offline support:** Yes

## What you can do

1. **Pixels, percentage or max dimension**
  Resize by exact pixels, scale percentage, or fit to a maximum side.
2. **Aspect-ratio lock**
  Preserve proportions by default, with an option to unlock.
3. **High-quality resampling**
  Canvas resampling with smoothing for sharp results.
4. **Runs entirely in your browser**
  Resizing and export are local; nothing is uploaded.

## How it works

The Image Resizer

Resize an image entirely in your browser. The source file and the resized result never leave your device.

## How it works

Upload an image, set target width and height in pixels or a percentage, lock or unlock aspect ratio, preview, then download.

## Privacy

Images are processed locally. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or logged.

## Limits

Very large images may be constrained by browser memory. Canvas-based processing does not preserve EXIF or ICC metadata. Transparency is preserved for PNG and WebP.
