Calculate a percentage of a number, what percentage one number is of another, percentage change, adding or subtracting a percentage, and percentage difference.

- **Zero handling:** Reported

## What you can do

1. **Six modes**
  Percentage of a number, what percentage, change, increase, decrease and difference.
2. **Clear terms**
  Percentage change and percentage difference are kept separate and labelled.
3. **Division by zero handled**
  Undefined cases are reported instead of returning infinity.
4. **Runs offline**
  All calculations are local.

## How it works

Percentage Calculator

One calculator for the percentage questions that come up constantly: what is a percentage of a number, what percentage one number is of another, how much a value increased or decreased, what happens when you add or remove a percentage, and the percentage difference between two values.

Percentage change and percentage difference are kept separate. The first measures a change from an original value; the second compares two values against their average.
