# Speed, Distance, and Time Calculator

Calculate speed, distance or time from the other two, with consistent unit conversion for metric and imperial units.

## At a glance

- **Modes:** 3
- **Distance units:** 5
- **Network requests:** None
- **Offline support:** Yes

## What you can do

1. **Three modes**
  Calculate speed, distance or time from the other two.
2. **Mixed units**
  Metric and imperial distance, speed and time units are converted consistently.
3. **Readable time**
  Long durations are shown as hours, minutes and seconds.
4. **Runs offline**
  All calculations are local.

## How it works

Speed, Distance, and Time Calculator

Given any two of speed, distance and time, the third follows from a single formula. Units are converted consistently before the calculation, so mixing kilometres, miles per hour and minutes is safe.

Results are mathematical estimates. Real journeys depend on traffic, terrain, stops and weather, none of which this calculator knows about.
