What you can do

  1. Number bases

    Convert between binary, octal, decimal and hexadecimal, with BigInt precision.

  2. Text and UTF-8

    Encode text to binary and decode binary back to UTF-8 text.

  3. Base64

  4. Arithmetic

  5. Bitwise and shifts

    AND, OR, XOR, NOT, and left and right shifts over a chosen bit width.

How it works

Binary Converter

One workspace for every binary task: number systems, text and UTF-8, Base64, arithmetic, bitwise logic and bit shifts. Every calculation runs as BigInt, so large values do not lose precision, and the browser handles the encoding so nothing leaves your device.

Number bases

Convert a value between binary, octal, decimal and hexadecimal by choosing the source and target base. Spaces and underscores are ignored on input, and a leading minus sign is kept through the conversion. The Swap button takes the result back as the next source.

Text and UTF-8

Text is encoded to bytes with the browser’s UTF-8 encoder, so emoji and non-Latin scripts produce the same bytes a server would. Binary input must contain whole 8-bit bytes; a trailing partial byte is reported rather than silently dropped.

Base64

Convert a byte stream between its binary form and standard or URL-safe Base64. Padding is added on the way back, and invalid Base64 is reported with a clear message instead of an empty result.

Arithmetic

Add, subtract, multiply, divide and take remainders of binary numbers. Results are signed when the operation produces a negative value, and division by zero is reported rather than returning infinity.

Bitwise operations

AND, OR, XOR and NOT over a chosen bit width, in either unsigned or signed two’s-complement form. Inputs are masked to the width, and the result is shown as a fixed-width bit pattern so leading zeros stay visible.

Shift operations

Left and right shifts, logical or arithmetic, over a chosen width. Shift amounts must be non-negative integers, and the result is always shown at the selected width.
