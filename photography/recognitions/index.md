# Recognized along the road: the photographs communities chose.

Photography groups and communities repeatedly selected these pictures to stand for their
days: photographs of the day and of the week, curated collections, group covers, gallery
features and excellence awards. Every photograph is shown with the organization that
recognized it, and with the banner or certificate exactly as it was published.
