# Photo Walk and Model Agencies

From 2016 through 2022, I organized photo walks and brought photographers and models together to create opportunities for practical experience, collaboration, and portfolio development - working across fashion photography in shops, studios, and outdoor locations, and participating in photography events associated with Sigma Model Agency while scouting for Liberty Model Agency.

**From 2016 through 2022, I organized photo walks and brought photographers and models together to create opportunities for practical experience, collaboration, and portfolio development.**

The initiative began as a way to help photographers learn how to work with models while building their portfolios. Models, in turn, could develop their own photographic portfolios and gain experience in front of the camera. What started as independent photo walks grew into a sustained collaborative practice spanning fashion photography in shops, studios, and outdoor locations.

## The Independent Initiative

The photo walks were organized independently, not as an agency program. I created the structure: finding locations, coordinating schedules, bringing photographers and models together. Each walk was a practical laboratory — photographers practiced direction, lighting, and composition with willing subjects; models built comfort in front of the camera and portfolio images they could use.

The format was deliberately simple. No formal contracts, no commercial obligations. Just photographers and models, meeting in a space, making photographs together. Over the years, dozens of photographers and models participated. Some returned repeatedly; others came once and took what they needed.

## Freelance Event Photography

Alongside the independent walks, I photographed events for Sigma Model Agency — fashion shows, castings, agency gatherings. These assignments ran parallel to the photo walks, separate in purpose but overlapping in community. The agency events were commercial work: delivering images to a client on a brief. The photo walks remained collaborative and educational.

## Model Scouting

During this period I also searched for models and emerging faces for Liberty Model Agency. This was scouting — identifying potential, introducing people to the agency, watching careers begin. It was a different kind of photography: less about the image, more about the person in front of it.

## The Camera

A Nikon D7200. It was the constant companion through the later years of this period. Many thousands of frames were made with it — photo walks, studio sessions, agency events, street portraits. The camera shaped the look: its rendering, its limitations, the way it forced certain choices about light and timing.

## The Project

**Photo Walk and Model Agencies** is a record of a collaborative photographic practice that ran for six years. It documents the intersection of independent education, freelance assignment work, and agency scouting — three strands that informed each other but were never the same thing.

The photographs are fragments of that practice: photographers learning to direct, models learning to be seen, studios and streets becoming classrooms, a D7200 recording it all.

This project exists as an archive of that period. The complete collection is considerably larger than the selection shown here.
