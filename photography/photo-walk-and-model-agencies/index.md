# Photo Walk and Model Agencies

From 2016 through 2019, I organized creative photo shoots and founded and organized photo walks, bringing photographers and models together to create opportunities for practical experience, collaboration, and portfolio development - working directly with models on creative shoots and other photography projects, providing freelance photography coverage for Sigma Models' fashion shows and agency events, and working as a freelance model scout for Liberty Model Agency.

**From 2016 through 2019, I organized creative photo shoots and founded and organized photo walks, bringing photographers and models together, and worked with two modeling agencies: Sigma Models and Liberty Model Agency.**

The initiative began as a way to help photographers learn how to work with models while building their portfolios. Models, in turn, could develop their own photographic portfolios and gain experience in front of the camera. What started as independent photo walks grew into a sustained collaborative practice: creative photo shoots with models, photography-related events I attended and hosted, and fashion photography in shops, studios, and outdoor locations.

## The Independent Initiative

The photo walks were organized independently, not as an agency program. I created the structure: finding locations, coordinating schedules, bringing photographers and models together. Each walk was a practical laboratory — photographers practiced direction, lighting, and composition with willing subjects; models built comfort in front of the camera and portfolio images they could use.

The format was deliberately simple. No formal contracts, no commercial obligations. Just photographers and models, meeting in a space, making photographs together. Alongside the walks I organized creative photo shoots and collaborated directly with models on creative shoots and other photography projects. Over the years, dozens of photographers and models participated. Some returned repeatedly; others came once and took what they needed.

## Sigma Models: Fashion Shows and Agency Events

Alongside the independent walks, I worked with Sigma Models (Sigma Model Agency): working as a photographer at the agency’s fashion shows and providing freelance photography coverage for various agency events and model-related activities. These assignments ran parallel to the photo walks, separate in purpose but overlapping in community. The agency events were commercial work: delivering images to a client on a brief. The photo walks remained collaborative and educational.

## Liberty Model Agency: Freelance Model Scouting

Also during this period I worked with Liberty Model Agency as a freelance model scout. I participated in fashion events, engaged with its communities and social media, and discovered and identified new faces and potential models for the agency. This was scouting, not shoot production: identifying potential, introducing people to the agency, watching careers begin. The two collaborations were related but distinct. Sigma Models was work as a photographer at agency events; Liberty Model Agency was scouting.

## The Camera

A Nikon D7200. It was the constant companion through the later years of this period. Many thousands of frames were made with it — photo walks, studio sessions, agency events, street portraits. The camera shaped the look: its rendering, its limitations, the way it forced certain choices about light and timing.

## The 2018–2019 Chapter

The 2018–2019 stretch of that period, the years of the available archive and testimonials, is documented in its own About chapter: [Photography, Creative Projects & Model Scouting](https://sergeyromanenko.com/about/photography-and-model-scouting/). It covers the Photo Walks community I founded and organized, event and fashion photography with Sigma Models, model scouting for Liberty Model Agency, the equipment I worked with and the people who took part. This page remains the record of the wider 2016–2019 initiative; the chapter is the closer look at its available archive and testimonials from 2018–2019.

## The Project

**Photo Walk and Model Agencies** is a record of a collaborative photographic practice that ran from 2016 through 2019. It documents the intersection of independent education, freelance assignment work, and agency scouting — three strands that informed each other but were never the same thing.

The photographs are fragments of that practice: photographers learning to direct, models learning to be seen, studios and streets becoming classrooms, a D7200 recording it all.

This project exists as an archive of that period. The complete collection is considerably larger than the selection shown here.
