# Tic-Tac-Toe

Play Tic-Tac-Toe against an optimal computer opponent or challenge a friend on the same device. A free, mobile-friendly browser game for quick breaks on trains, airplanes, and during travel.

## At a glance

- **Game modes:** 2
- **AI difficulty:** Optimal (minimax)
- **Players:** 1 or 2
- **Offline support:** Yes

## What you can do

1. **Play against an optimal computer opponent**
  The computer uses a minimax algorithm and will never lose against correct play.
2. **Two players on one device**
  Take turns as X and O on the same phone or laptop — perfect for travel.
3. **Fully keyboard accessible**
  Navigate with arrow keys, play with Enter or Space, jump with Home/End.
4. **Works offline**
  Runs entirely in your browser with no network requests after load — play on airplanes and trains.
5. **Scores persist locally**
  X wins, O wins, and draws are saved to localStorage and survive page reloads.

## How it works

Take a quick break wherever your journey takes you. Play Tic-Tac-Toe against the computer or challenge a friend on the same device. No download or account required. Load the game before traveling and enjoy it offline once its required files are cached.

## Game Modes

### vs Computer

You play **X**, the computer plays **O**. The computer uses a minimax algorithm to play optimally — it will never lose against a correctly played human opponent. When multiple equally good moves exist, it chooses among them for varied gameplay.

### Two Players

Two people take turns on the same device. Players alternate as **X** and **O**. The active player is clearly indicated. Switching modes starts a new game.

## How to Play

1. **Choose your mode** — Play against the computer or another person
2. **Tap an empty cell** — Place your mark (X or O)
3. **Get three in a row** — Horizontal, vertical, or diagonal wins
4. **Full board without a winner** — It’s a draw
5. **Press Restart** — Play again instantly

## Keyboard Controls

The game is fully keyboard accessible:

- **Arrow keys** — Navigate between cells
- **Enter / Space** — Place your mark
- **Home / End** — Jump to first/last empty cell

## Offline Play

The game runs entirely in your browser with no network requests after the initial load. Once you’ve visited the page, it works offline for travel — on airplanes, trains, or anywhere without connectivity.

## Technical Details

- **Algorithm**: Minimax with alpha-beta pruning (optimal play)
- **Storage**: Scores and mode preference saved to localStorage
- **Accessibility**: WCAG AA compliant, screen reader friendly, keyboard navigable
- **Mobile-first**: Responsive board with 48px+ touch targets
- **No dependencies**: Pure Vue 3 + TypeScript, no external libraries

- **Access:** Free to play, no account
