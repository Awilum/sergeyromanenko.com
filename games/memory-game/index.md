# Memory Game

Test your memory with this classic card matching game. Multiple difficulties, themes, and game modes. Play offline on flights, trains, and during travel.

## At a glance

- **Difficulties:** 3
- **Themes:** 6
- **Game modes:** 3
- **Offline support:** Yes

## What you can do

1. **Three difficulty levels**
  Easy (4×4), Medium (4×6), and Hard (6×6) boards for all skill levels.
2. **Six beautiful themes**
  Emojis, Nature, Animals, Food, Travel, and Symbols — choose your favorite.
3. **Three game modes**
  Classic, Timed Challenge, and Limited Moves — each with configurable options.
4. **Preview mode**
  Optional 3-second preview of all cards before the game begins.
5. **Hints and streak bonuses**
  Limited hints to reveal pairs; consecutive matches earn bonus points.
6. **Works offline**
  Runs entirely in your browser with no network requests after load — play on airplanes and trains.
7. **Scores and records persist**
  Best scores, preferences, and records saved to localStorage.

## How it works

Take a quick break wherever your journey takes you. Test your memory with this classic card matching game (Concentration). Multiple difficulty levels, beautiful themes, and game modes. No download or account required. Load the game before traveling and enjoy it offline once its required files are cached.

## Game Modes

### Classic

Find all matching pairs at your own pace. No time limit, no move limit. Perfect for relaxing play.

### Timed Challenge

Race against the clock! Find all pairs before time runs out. Choose from 30, 60, 90, or 120 second limits.

### Limited Moves

Complete the puzzle within a set number of moves. Every move counts. Choose from 15, 20, 30, or 40 move limits.

## Card Themes

Choose from six beautiful themes:

- **Emojis** — Classic smileys and expressions
- **Nature** — Trees, flowers, leaves
- **Animals** — Cute creatures from around the world
- **Food** — Delicious fruits and dishes
- **Travel** — Planes, trains, and vehicles
- **Symbols** — Hearts, peace signs, and more

## Features

- **Preview Mode** — See all cards for 3 seconds before the game starts
- **Hints** — Reveal a matching pair (limited uses per game)
- **Streak Bonus** — Consecutive matches increase your score multiplier
- **Move Counter** — Track your efficiency
- **Timer** — See how fast you can complete the board
- **Best Score** — Personal records saved per difficulty
- **Keyboard Support** — Play entirely with number keys (1-9) and shortcuts

## How to Play

1. **Choose your settings** — Difficulty, theme, mode, and options
2. **Start the game** — Cards are shuffled and laid face down
3. **Flip two cards** — Click or tap to reveal them
4. **Match pairs** — If they match, they stay face up; if not, they flip back
5. **Clear the board** — Find all pairs to win!

## Keyboard Controls

- **1-9** — Quick flip cards by position
- **Space** — Restart current game
- **P** — Pause/Resume
- **H** — Use hint
- **R** — Restart

## Offline Play

The game runs entirely in your browser with no network requests after the initial load. Once you’ve visited the page, it works offline for travel — on airplanes, trains, or anywhere without connectivity.

## Technical Details

- **Algorithm**: Fisher-Yates shuffle for fair randomization
- **Storage**: Scores, preferences, and best records saved to localStorage
- **Accessibility**: WCAG AA compliant, screen reader friendly, keyboard navigable
- **Mobile-first**: Responsive grid with touch-friendly cards
- **No dependencies**: Pure Vue 3 + TypeScript, no external libraries
