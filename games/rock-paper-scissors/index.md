# Rock Paper Scissors

Play Rock Paper Scissors against the computer. A free, mobile-friendly browser game for quick breaks on trains, airplanes, and during travel.

## At a glance

- **Choices:** 3
- **Rounds:** Unlimited
- **Offline support:** Yes

## What you can do

1. **Play against the computer**
  Choose Rock, Paper, or Scissors — the computer picks randomly for fair play.
2. **Instant results**
  See the outcome immediately with clear visual feedback for both choices.
3. **Running score**
  Track your wins, computer wins, and draws across multiple rounds.
4. **Works offline**
  Runs entirely in your browser with no network requests after load — play on airplanes and trains.
5. **Scores persist locally**
  Wins, losses, and draws are saved to localStorage and survive page reloads.

## How it works

Take a quick break wherever your journey takes you. Play Rock Paper Scissors against the computer. No download or account required. Load the game before traveling and enjoy it offline once its required files are cached.

## How to Play

1. **Choose your move** — Rock, Paper, or Scissors
2. **Computer responds** — The computer randomly chooses its move
3. **See the result** — Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock
4. **Track your score** — Wins, losses, and draws are counted
5. **Play again** — Press “Play Again” for another round

## Rules

- **Rock** crushes **Scissors**
- **Scissors** cuts **Paper**
- **Paper** covers **Rock**
- Same choice = Draw

## Keyboard Controls

The game is fully keyboard accessible:

- **Tab** — Navigate between choices
- **Enter / Space** — Select the focused choice
- **Enter / Space** on “Play Again” — Start next round

## Offline Play

The game runs entirely in your browser with no network requests after the initial load. Once you’ve visited the page, it works offline for travel — on airplanes, trains, or anywhere without connectivity.

## Technical Details

- **Algorithm**: Random computer choice (fair play)
- **Storage**: Scores and draws saved to localStorage
- **Accessibility**: WCAG AA compliant, screen reader friendly, keyboard navigable
- **Mobile-first**: Responsive layout with touch-friendly buttons
- **No dependencies**: Pure Vue 3 + TypeScript, no external libraries

## Details

- **Access:** Free to play, no account
- **Runs in:** Web
- **Year:** 2026
