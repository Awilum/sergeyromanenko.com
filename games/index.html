Games for Your Journey.

7 games · 7 tags · No account, no tracking

Lightweight browser games built for travel — play offline on flights, trains, and layovers. No accounts, no downloads, no tracking. Load them before you go and they stay ready whenever the journey has a pause.

These games grew out of the same need that shaped the tools: a long journey with spotty connectivity, a desire for something to do that doesn't demand a network connection or an account. Each game runs entirely in your browser — the code, the logic, the scores all live on your device. Open the page once while you have a connection, and it stays playable for the rest of the trip.

Privacy & Security

Your Data Stays on Your Device

Every game runs entirely in your browser — no uploads, no accounts, no tracking. Scores and preferences stay in your local storage.
