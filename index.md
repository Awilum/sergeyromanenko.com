# Go out into the world. Look closer. Discover what lies beneath. Make something worth sharing.

— Sergey Romanenko

I work in two places at once. There is the physical world, which I get to know by walking through it for a long time with a camera, and there is the built one, which I get to know by taking apart. Software came first, but it never stopped — and neither did the teaching, the writing, or the road.

How I work

## There is no method. Just a few *habits* that keep returning.

1. 01
  ### Go and see
  Start with the thing itself — the road, the codebase, the person trying to learn. Look before deciding what it means.
2. 02
  ### Look beneath the surface
  Understand what makes things work the way they do. The mechanisms behind a system, the circumstances behind a photograph, the ideas behind a difficult concept.
3. 03
  ### Make something meaningful
  A photograph that notices something. A course that makes difficult ideas usable. A book that rewards attention. A tool that solves a real problem.
4. 04
  ### Put it into the world
  Build it, refine it, publish it, share it. Let the work find its purpose beyond the moment it was made.
